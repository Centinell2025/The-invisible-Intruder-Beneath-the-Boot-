# Case brief — Beneath the Boot
Northbridge Systems is a fictional research company. On 2026-09-17 a Windows workstation (NB-WS-014) generated an integrity alert shortly after an authorized maintenance window. The security team suspects an attacker attempted to hide behind legitimate service activity.

Your task is to establish a defensible timeline, distinguish documented maintenance from anomalous activity, and decide whether evidence supports a firmware-level compromise, a policy/configuration change, or an unresolved finding.

**Rules:** Use only supplied evidence; do not assume alerts are ground truth. Record evidence filename and event ID for every conclusion. All data is synthetic. The workstation is not live.
