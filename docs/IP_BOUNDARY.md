# CoreShield intellectual property boundary
The private manuscript *Centinell CoreShield — Defensa Cibernética del Núcleo Estratégico* is **reference material only** and must never be committed, bundled, excerpted, or uploaded to the challenge platform without a separate explicit authorization.

The case uses independent, synthetic DFIR evidence and publicly understood concepts: boot integrity, firmware inventory, Secure Boot, TPM/PCR measurements, chain of custody, and authentication controls.

No proprietary commands, algorithms, internal diagrams, trade secrets, private IP addresses, credentials, or real customer data may enter the student or reviewer bundles.

Maintain instructor solutions in restricted access and export student packages with an explicit allowlist.
